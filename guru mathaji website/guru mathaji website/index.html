About Mathaji

0+ Years of Service

Shri Samayakka Horamma Jyotidar Nilayam is a revered spiritual master, a living embodiment of divine grace whose life and teachings have illuminated countless souls across the globe.

From her earliest years, Shri Sammakka Saralamma Astrology was drawn to the profound depths of meditation and spiritual inquiry. Through unwavering devotion, intense sadhana, and the grace of the Supreme, She ascended to the highest states of spiritual realization.

With boundless compassion, Shri Sammakka Saralamma Astrology guides seekers on the path of dharma, self-discovery, and inner transformation. Her teachings weave the ancient wisdom of the Vedas, Upanishads, and Bhagavad Gita into practical guidance for the modern soul.
